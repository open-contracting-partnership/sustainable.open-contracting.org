    <p class="notion-text notion-text__content notion-semantic-string">In 2005 Green Public Procurement (GPP) started to be actively promoted in the Government of Thailand. <strong>As part of the GPP strategy, a list for green public procurement products called </strong><strong><a href="http://gp.pcd.go.th/" class="notion-link link">“Green Cart”</a></strong><strong> was developed.</strong> The Green Cart is both a catalogue and a set of criteria based on the Thai <a href="https://www.tei.or.th/greenlabel/en/" class="notion-link link">Green Label</a> (for products) and the <a href="http://www.greenleafthai.org/en/" class="notion-link link">Green Leaf</a> (for hotels). Green Cart Criteria covers 17 products, and 5 services.

Each procuring agency can check whether procuring products satisfy the Green Cart criteria. <strong>These criteria are usually set as essential requirements, and award decisions are mainly based on the lowest cost. </strong></p>
