  <div class="notion-callout__content"><span class="notion-semantic-string"><strong><a href="https://ec.europa.eu/docsroom/documents/45767" class="notion-link link">Copenhagen has established a task force</a></strong><strong> for monitoring compliance with labor clauses</strong> of all suppliers in construction projects, and obtaining relevant data. 

The task force consists of <strong>nine people, and is entitled to visit all types of workplaces in Denmark at all times</strong>, monitoring pay and working conditions. The focus is set in specific sectors, including construction, cleaning and transport. They combine on-the-spot inspections - which represent around one third of total inspections- with investigations into the conditions of specific employees based on documentation of pay, working hours, tax, etc.

<strong>The task force carries out around 600 compliance checks each year. </strong></span></div>
