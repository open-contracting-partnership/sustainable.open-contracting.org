In 2005, the Government of The Netherlands set up [PIANOo](https://www.pianoo.nl/en), **a knowledge network for government procurement officers and contracting authorities.** PIANOo was created with the aim of professionalizing procurement, seeking to improve efficiency and compliance.

It also now incorporates a specific section on Sustainable Public Procurement (SPP), offering learning materials, guidance, **and a [criteria tool](https://www.mvicriteria.nl/en) for public authorities to identify possible environmental requirements to include in tendering processes.**

The expertise center includes information regarding best practice exchange meetings amongst procurement practitioners.
